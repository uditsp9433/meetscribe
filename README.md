## 🗒️ What is meetscribe?

meetscribe is a simple tool that helps you create clear meeting transcripts. It listens to your recorded meetings and writes down what was said. Then, it identifies who spoke when. It also uses AI to summarize the main points. You can save everything as a PDF for easy sharing.

Everything runs fully on your own computer. This means your meeting audios stay private and you don’t need an internet connection to use it.

## 🎙️ Using meetscribe to Transcribe Meetings

Steps to transcribe your meeting recordings:

1. Click the **Load Audio** button.

2. Select your meeting file from your computer (MP3, WAV, or M4A).

3. Click **Start Transcription**.

4. meetscribe will analyze the audio. This might take a few minutes depending on file length.

5. You'll see the transcript appear on the screen as text.

6. The program will also identify different speakers and mark their parts.

---

## 📝 Summaries and PDF Output

After transcription:

1. Go to the **Summary** tab in meetscribe.

2. Click **Generate Summary**.

3. The AI will create a brief list of main points from the discussion.

4. To save your work, click **Export as PDF**.

5. Choose where to save the file on your computer.

6. The PDF will include the full transcript, speaker labels, and the summary.

## ⚙️ Settings and Preferences

- **Output folder:** Change where your PDFs save by default.

You can access these from the **Settings** menu, found at the top right of the app window.
